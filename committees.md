## About the Steering Committee

#### Role of the SC
The role of the steering committee (SC) is to set the long-term direction
of the QCTiP conference, to decide on locations for the next events, to appoint
the PC Chair and invited speakers, to advise on policies relating to paper
submission rules and to approve the structure of the schedule/programme for
the next event. The SC should also serve as a watchdog to oversee the work of
the local organisers and advise on the dates and structure of the programme of
the next QCTiP event.

#### Members of the SC
The SC consists of approximately 6 members. A new SC with a new SC Chair is
appointed every year within 2 months after the most recent QCTiP event.
The SC typically includes the local organizers of the previous three QCTiP
conferences, the local organiser(s) of the next QCTiP event, and is chaired
by one of the local organisers of the most recent previous event.
SC members are expected to serve typically for 3 years.

#### Appointing leadership for future events
At every QCTiP event, the SC invites members of the community to submit bids
for holding the next QCTiP conference. A bid should indicate the proposed venue and names of
the proposed lead organisers.

The newly appointed SC then considers these bids, approves the
next location for QCTiP, and appoints the local organisers.
Starting typically in summer, the SC also selects the Chair of the Programme Committee
for the next QCTiP conference and additionally invites a small number of invited speakers
(of the best recent research, but possibly also some more perspective/survey talks).


The SC may additionally advise the Programme Committee Chair on policies relating
to paper submission rules etc, but otherwise gives freedom to the PC on
setting the conference programme. The SC may also advise the local organisers
on the structure of the schedule/programme for the next event before approving it.
